A trick shot game for iPhone and iPad

Crazy ElephantPop every bird in fewer shots

An elephant fires bowling balls into the sky, and every bird up there has to pop. Aim, set the power, fire, and pop them all in as few shots as possible. 80 hand-made levels, and new levels keep coming.

Free. No ads. No account needed.

How it works

ClearPop enough birds to clear the level and earn a star.

All birdsPop every bird for two stars.

ParPop every bird within par for three stars.

Features

Aim, set the power, fire

Touch anywhere to aim and drag for power. Bounce off bouncers, crack crumbling walls, and hit bowling birds that drop an extra ball when they pop. Superballs are smaller and faster.

Beat your personal best

Fewer shots, then faster time. Every level keeps your best run, and a run within par earns all three stars. Pop the golden bird too, within gold par, to earn gold. All birds down with shots to spare? Keep going for the golden bird.

So close? Use an extra ball and finish the level.

Every level at a glance

The level grid shows your stars, your gold, your best and the par of each level, so you see right away where a star is still missing.

Build your own levels

Place birds, walls, bouncers, portals and wind zones on a grid, give it a number of balls, then press Play and try it.

My Levels

Your own levels wait in My Levels. Rename, duplicate, edit or play them, and your fewest shots become the level's par.

Your progress, on every device

Progress and extra balls sync across your iPhone and iPad with iCloud. Stats count every bird you popped, every shot and every golden bird.

Every piece in the game

Fourteen pieces make up the levels. The Pieces guide in the game shows each one.

Bird

Pops when a ball flies through it. Birds never block a ball.

Bowling bird

Drops an extra ball when it pops.

Wall

Stops the ball. A ball that lands on top slides off.

Crumbling wall

Stops the ball once, then crumbles away.

Bouncer

Sends the ball back at full speed. The only way to send a ball up again.

Big bouncer

A tall bouncer for long bank shots.

S

Superball

Smaller and faster than a normal ball.

Moving wall

Portal

A ball that flies into one ring comes out of the other, just as fast and in the same direction.

Bomb bird

Armored bird

The first hit knocks off its helmet, the second pops the bird.

Wind zone

Golden bird

Rare and optional. Every one you pop is collected. Pop it and every bird within gold par for gold.

Questions

What does it cost?

Crazy Elephant is free to download, and Pack 1 is free to play. Earn Pack 2, Pack 3 and Pack 4 with stars, or buy Unlock All Packs to open every level pack, now and later. Extra balls (1 or 5) are optional in-app purchases.

Are there ads?

No. There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics.

Do I need an account?

No. Start the app and play. Sign in to Game Center only if you want leaderboards and achievements.

Does my progress sync?

Yes. Your progress and your extra balls sync across your devices with iCloud, through your own iCloud account.

Will there be more levels?

Yes. New levels keep coming in updates. Unlock All Packs includes them.

Which languages?

English, German and Spanish.

Which devices?

Made for iPhone and iPad, with iOS 17 or later.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Every level and the level editor work without a connection. iCloud sync and Game Center need one.
