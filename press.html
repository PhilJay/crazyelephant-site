Short description

Crazy Elephant is a trick-shot puzzle game for iPhone and iPad. First put on the App Store in 2013, it has now been rebuilt from scratch: an elephant fires bowling balls into the sky, and every bird up there has to pop, in as few shots as possible.

Long description

Crazy Elephant was first put on the App Store in 2013. Now it has been rebuilt from scratch for today's iPhone and iPad, with new art, new pieces and a new way to score.

The idea stayed simple. An elephant fires bowling balls into the sky, and every bird up there has to pop. Players aim, set the power and fire, banking shots off bouncers, cracking crumbling walls and hitting bowling birds that drop an extra ball when they pop.

Every level is scored like golf. Clearing it earns a star, popping every bird earns two, and doing it within par earns three. A rare golden bird hides in each level for players who want gold. A par solver checks every official level, so every par can really be reached.

The game has 80 hand-made levels in four packs, a Playground to learn the shots, and Spooky Night with 20 Halloween levels. A level editor lets players build their own levels, and their fewest shots become the par. There are no ads, no account and no tracking, and every level works offline.

Features

  • Aim, set the power, fire: one-touch controls on iPhone and iPad
  • Par and gold par on every level, plus a hidden golden bird
  • 14 classic pieces, from bouncers and portals to bomb birds, armored birds and wind zones
  • Spooky Night: ghost birds, bats, pumpkins and dark levels lit by lanterns
  • Level editor and My Levels
  • Game Center leaderboards for total stars and the fewest shots on every level
  • 43 achievements
  • Progress, extra balls and unused hints sync with iCloud
  • English, German and Spanish
